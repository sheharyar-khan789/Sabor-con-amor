## Brand & Style

This design system mediates between high-ceremony luxury hospitality and precise enterprise orchestration. It serves three distinct user mindsets across a single cohesive architectural framework:
- **Public & Patron Experience:** Grandeur, cultural prestige, warm spatial opulence, and romantic certainty for hosts selecting a marquee venue.
- **Client Planning Portal:** Calm, reassuring clarity that demystifies seating charts, stage themes, vendor contracts, and tasting schedules.
- **Operational Back-Office SaaS:** Dense, high-legibility, distraction-free control panels that manage multi-hall slot turnarounds, guest tallies, power redundancies, and catering milestones.

The visual direction blends **Heritage Modernism** and **Restrained Editorial Luxury**:
- Architectural balance over decorative excess: Generous negative space, structured classical symmetry, and crisp division lines echoing Mughal colonnades and contemporary glass marquees.
- Tactile warmth: Off-white champagne and ivory base planes replace sterile digital grays, creating an ambiance of silk linens, brass fixtures, and polished marble.
- High-precision utility: Micro-interactions remain subtle, measured, and swift to ensure administrative power without sacrificing celebratory elevation.

## Colors

The palette balances deep botanical emeralds with warm metallic brass and breathable natural grounds:

- **Primary (`#0F2E24`) & Secondary Primary (`#184235`):** Imperial Emerald. Communicates institutional heritage, stability, and quiet authority. Used for prominent CTA buttons, core navigations, top-level headings, and active state highlights.
- **Accent Metallic Gold (`#C5A059` base, `#DFBD78` highlight, `#9A7736` deep):** Antique Gold. Reserved for subtle hairline badges, elevated borders, focused states, and key monetary/VIP tier identifiers. Never used indiscriminately as background fills.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals (`#FAF8F5` base canvas, `#F5F1EA` elevated card canvas, `#EBE5DA` muted container):** Raw silk and ivory tones that preserve warmth under bright office displays or mobile outdoor sunlight.
- **Typography & Ink (`#1A211E` main body, `#2D3732` secondary muted, `#5C6661` captions/placeholders):** Deep onyx-slate with an organic green-tint undertone, avoiding harsh `#000000` contrasts.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8E4` default hairline, `#D0D9D3` strong outline, `#C5A059`/30 hover accent):** Muted sage hairlines that separate functional tables and modules cleanly.
- **Operational Status Palette:**
  - *Confirmed / Settled:* Deep Emerald (`#134E39` on `#E7F4EE`)
  - *Pending / Enquiry:* Warm Honey Amber (`#92600E` on `#FEF6E7`)
  - *In Review / Planning:* Muted Sapphire (`#1D4E89` on `#EDF4FC`)
  - *Cancelled / Alert:* Velvet Crimson (`#8A2424` on `#FDF0F0`)

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between celebratory tradition and pragmatic SaaS utility:
- **Headings & Display Roles (`Playfair Display`):** Used strictly for high-visibility visual anchors—venue names, package titles, marquee hall banners, and macro dashboard greetings. It communicates classical grace without compromising scanning speeds.
- **Body, Inputs, Metrics, and UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides immaculate clarity in complex reservation grids, invoice line-items, and guest lists. The neutral yet warm geometric construction retains readability down to 11px micro-labels.
- **Tabular Figures:** Numbers in budgets, pax capacities, and date schedulers must utilize tabular lining (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`) to eliminate horizontal jitter across dense rows.
- **Uppercase Restraint:** Labels and section kickers using uppercase letters must always employ tracked spacing (`letterSpacing: 0.05em` to `0.08em`) to preserve legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances sprawling editorial breathing room for showcase views with strict mathematical rhythm for scheduling software:

- **Grid System:**
  - **Desktop (12 Columns, 1440px target):** 2.5rem outer canvas margin, 1.5rem column gutters. Administrative console sidebars lock to a fixed 280px left rail, with the main content area expanding fluidly with a maximum operational content bound of 1600px.
  - **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px - 1024px):** 1.5rem margin, 1rem gutter. Operational tables convert to sticky horizontally swipeable views with primary metrics pinned.
  - **Mobile (4 Columns, 375px - 767px):** 1rem margin, 0.75rem gutter. Sidebars collapse to bottom navigation or off-canvas drawers; venue cards and booking timelines shift to a clean vertical feed.
- **Density Scaling:**
  - Public marketing and venue exploration adopt expansive rhythm (`space-lg` to `space-xl` element gaps).
  - Back-office operational interfaces (slot matrices, guest lists, kitchen sheets) step down one increment (`space-xs` to `space-md`) to ensure critical operational metrics remain above the fold without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined architectural technique: **layered parchment planes paired with subtle warm ambient dissipation and hairline borders**. Harsh, cold drop shadows are prohibited.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` (Warm Ivory). Ground plane for the entire viewport.
  - **Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Structural Panels):** `#F5F1EA` with a `1px solid #E2E8E4` perimeter border. Flat depth, distinguished primarily through border definition and surface tonal shift.
  - **Tier 2 (Popovers, Filter Menus, Date Pickers, Hovered Elements):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 46, 36, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)` and border `1px solid #D0D9D3`.
  - **Tier 3 (Modals, Seating Layout Canvas Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 46, 36, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.20)`. Backdrops apply a warm charcoal tint (`rgba(26, 33, 30, 0.6)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- **Understated Gold Micro-Glow:**
  - Active interactive cards and highlighted primary interactions emit an ambient gold rim: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #C5A059, 0 4px 12px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. This balances traditional architectural stonework with precise software execution:

- **Default UI Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Checkboxes, Chips):** `0.25rem` (4px). Crisp, formal, tailored, and sturdy.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Softened just enough to avoid brittle sharpness, while preventing excessive consumer-app roundness.
- **Dialogs, Large Modals, Showcase Media Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Status Pills and Badges:** Full-pill (`9999px`) styling is permitted *only* for compact state badges (e.g., Confirmed, In Review, VIP Hall) to set them apart from actionable controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Action:** Solid Emerald Green (`#0F2E24`), text `#FAF8F5`, `0.25rem` radius, `1px solid #0F2E24`. Hover: Background shifts to `#184235` with micro-glow `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(15, 46, 36, 0.2), 0 0 0 1px #C5A059`. Padding: `0.625rem 1.25rem`.
- **Secondary / Brass Accent:** Transparent background, `1px solid #C5A059`, text `#0F2E24`. Hover: Background `#FAF8F5` with border `#9A7736` and subtle gold tint.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Neutral canvas, transparent border, text `#2D3732`. Hover: Background `#EBE5DA` with text `#0F2E24`.
- **Admin Table Micro-Action:** Compact `28px` height, `0.25rem` radius, subtle `#E2E8E4` border, text `#2D3732`, font-size `12px`.

### Form Controls & Input Fields
- **Input Fields & Selects:** Canvas `#FFFFFF`, hairline border `1px solid #D0D9D3`, text `#1A211E`, placeholder `#5C6661`. Height: `42px` (Public/Client), `34px` (SaaS Admin).
- **Focused State:** Border transitions to `#C5A059` with a subtle focus ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.18)`. Never generic browser blue.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px x 16px`. Default border `1px solid #D0D9D3`. Selected state: `#0F2E24` fill with `#FAF8F5` checkmark or center pip.

### Chips & Status Pills
- **Status Indicators:** Height `24px`, padding `0 10px`, `roundedness: 9999px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, letter-spacing `0.04em`, uppercase.
  - *Confirmed / Active:* Background `#E7F4EE`, text `#134E39`, border `1px solid rgba(19, 78, 57, 0.15)`.
  - *Pending / Enquiry:* Background `#FEF6E7`, text `#92600E`, border `1px solid rgba(146, 96, 14, 0.15)`.
  - *In Review:* Background `#EDF4FC`, text `#1D4E89`, border `1px solid rgba(29, 78, 137, 0.15)`.
  - *Cancelled / Void:* Background `#FDF0F0`, text `#8A2424`, border `1px solid rgba(138, 36, 36, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Layout Modules
- **Venue & Showcase Cards:** Grounded `#F5F1EA` surface, bordered with `1px solid #E2E8E4`, `0.5rem` radius. Media containers feature a `16:9` or `4:3` ratio with an internal hairline divider. Hover applies subtle `-2px` translateY and a gold-tinted hairline border.
- **Operational Data Widgets (SaaS):** Clean `#FFFFFF` surface, compact padding (`1rem`), minimal borders (`#E2E8E4`), integrated KPI badges, and neutral `#FAF8F5` metric containers.

### Tables & Admin Grids
- **Header Cells:** Background `#F5F1EA`, text `#2D3732`, font size `11px`, bold uppercase, letter spacing `0.06em`, border-bottom `1px solid #D0D9D3`.
- **Row Styling:** Alternate subtle row striping (`#FFFFFF` to `#FAF8F5`), row height `44px` (regular) or `36px` (compact), border-bottom `1px solid #E2E8E4`.
- **Hover Row:** Background shifts to `#F5F1EA` with an emerald indicator on the leftmost edge (`3px solid #0F2E24`).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Hall Slot Calendar Matrix:** Multi-hall schedule ribbon displaying day-part blocks (Matinee / Evening / Barat / Walima) with emerald capacity gauges, status pills, and gold conflict flags.
- **Stage & Seating Canvas Navigator:** CAD-style layout view bordered in `#D0D9D3` with zoom-lock controls, stage elevation indicators, and table allocation capacity counters.
- **Catering & Menu Course Stepper:** Editorial tabbed sequence tracking Welcome Drinks, Main Course, and Dessert services with live per-head cost recalculations.
